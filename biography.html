Biography

Born in France in 1986, Annick Vavasseur is a contemporary painter whose work explores memory, perception, and what she refers to as her “inner landscapes”.

Her background in literature, art history, and traditional painting techniques informs a practice that moves between material sensitivity and conceptual inquiry.

She studied Visual Arts Research at Panthéon-Sorbonne University and continues to develop her practice through drawing, painting, and observational work within Paris-based institutions, including the Louvre Museum, Paris Ateliers, and the Ateliers des Beaux-Arts de la Ville de Paris.

Her work explores the tension between lived experience and imagined space, where painting becomes a site of memory and transformation.
