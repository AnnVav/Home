Biography

Born in France in 1986, Annick Vavasseur is a contemporary painter whose work explores intangible worlds and the unseen through inner landscapes. Color occupies a central place in her work, becoming both a material language and a vehicle for perception.

Her practice is informed by studies in literature, art history, and painting techniques, developing a visual language that brings together material exploration with questions of memory, perception, and lived experience.

She studied Visual Arts Research at Panthéon-Sorbonne University and continues to develop her practice through trompe l'oeil techniques, drawing, painting, and observational studies at Paris-based institutions, including the Louvre Museum, Paris Ateliers, and the Ateliers des Beaux-Arts de la Ville de Paris

Her paintings have been presented in selected exhibitions in France and internationally. They investigate the relationship between lived experience and imagined space, inhabiting the threshold between the visible and the invisible, where painting becomes a psychological environment and a site of transmutation.
