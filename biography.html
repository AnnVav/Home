Biography

Born in France in 1986, Annick Vavasseur is a contemporary painter whose work explores intangible worlds and the unseen through inner landscapes. Color occupies a central place in her practice, becoming both a material language and a means of perception.

Her artistic approach is informed by studies in literature, art history, and painting techniques, gradually developing a visual language that brings together material exploration with questions of memory, perception, and lived experience.

She studied Visual Arts at Panthéon-Sorbonne University and continues to develop her artistic research through drawing, painting, trompe l’œil techniques, and observational studies at Paris-based institutions, including the Louvre Museum, Paris Ateliers, and the Ateliers des Beaux-Arts de la Ville de Paris.

Her paintings have been presented in selected exhibitions in France and internationally. They investigate the relationship between lived experience and imagined space, inhabiting the threshold between the visible and the invisible, where painting becomes a psychological environment and a space of transformation.
