Artist Statement

The first Inner Landscape appeared unexpectedly in 2015. While preparing the background of a painting, a landscape began to emerge — not from intention or imagination, not from a preconceived plan, but from something more instinctive.

It was as though the painting revealed itself through the gesture. Images surfaced slowly, unexpected yet unmistakable, as if they had always been there, waiting to appear.

Since then, painting has become less a way of representing the visible world than of exploring what lies beneath it. Each work begins with an intuition rather than a narrative. Some forms are sought, others emerge unexpectedly, as painting itself allows uncertainty, discovery and revision in a way other media don't.

Instead of revealing everything at once, I leave traces, subtle details, and quiet ambiguities that invite contemplation and observation.

Rather than revealing themselves immediately, the paintings unfold slowly. Small presences, shifting forms and quiet ambiguities emerge over time, rewarding sustained attention.

Built through successive layers, my paintings bring together fragments of memory, emotion, imagined landscapes, and lived experience. Over time, they continue to unfold through contemplation, inviting sustained attention.
